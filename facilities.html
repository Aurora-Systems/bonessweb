Our Home

Purpose-Built for Comfort & Dignity

Bo'ness Care Home was designed from the ground up with residents' needs at the heart of every decision. From the light-filled corridors to the mature private gardens, every detail creates an environment where people feel genuinely at home.

Our 27 en-suite rooms , primarily single occupancy , provide privacy and personal space, while our shared areas foster the social connections and community that make life meaningful.

Outdoors

Private Grounds & Gardens

Bo'ness Care Home is set within beautiful mature private grounds. The traditional bandstand is a beloved feature of the gardens, and residents enjoy outdoor activities, walking, and simply relaxing in the fresh air throughout the seasons.

Our large car park ensures visitors can always find a space, and the peaceful grounds provide a wonderful backdrop for family visits and social gatherings.

Wellness

Salon & Wellbeing Suite

We understand that looking good is feeling good. Our on-site hairdressing salon and wellbeing suite offer residents professional services without leaving the comfort of their home.

From haircuts and styling to chiropody and complementary therapies, our visiting professionals bring high-quality care directly to our residents.
