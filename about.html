Who We Are

Purpose-Built for Exceptional Care

Bo'ness Care Home is a purpose-built nursing facility located in the heart of Bo'ness, Scotland. From our very foundation, we have been guided by a single principle: that every person deserves to live with dignity, comfort, and genuine happiness.

Our 27 en-suite rooms — predominantly single occupancy — provide a private, personal space for each resident. Two welcoming lounges, a quiet sitting room, and a separate dining room create the social fabric of daily life, while our mature private gardens offer peace and beauty in every season.

We are proud to be registered and regulated by the Care Inspectorate of Scotland, and our internal auditing procedures ensure we consistently meet and exceed the highest standards.

Our Values

What Guides Everything We Do

These four pillars shape every interaction, every care plan, and every decision we make.

Compassion

Every resident receives genuine kindness and empathy from our staff — because caring is more than a profession, it's a calling.

Dignity

We honour the individuality of each resident, protecting their privacy, respecting their choices, and preserving their sense of self.

Excellence

Rigorous internal standards and regular auditing ensure we consistently deliver nursing care of the highest quality.

Community

Bo'ness Care Home thrives as a community — residents, families, and staff working together to create a warm, joyful home.

Our Location

Ideally Located in Bo'ness

Situated on Bridgeness Lane, our home enjoys a peaceful setting close to Bo'ness town centre. Residents can easily access local shops, amenities, and public transport, maintaining the connections that matter to them.

Our large car park ensures visitors can come easily at any time, and our grounds offer a beautiful outdoor environment to enjoy year-round.
