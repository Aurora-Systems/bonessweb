Our Care Services

Comprehensive, compassionate care tailored to every individual , delivered with professionalism and warmth.

Our Approach

Care That Centres on You

At Bo'ness Care Home, we believe that outstanding care begins with truly understanding each person. We take the time to know our residents , their histories, preferences, needs, and wishes , building care plans that reflect the whole person, not just a medical profile.

Every plan is regularly reviewed and adjusted in partnership with residents and their families, ensuring our care evolves as needs change. Our qualified nursing team is available around the clock, supported by a wider team of carers, lifestyle coordinators, and wellbeing professionals.

What We Offer

Services Designed
Around Each Resident

01

Nursing Care

Our qualified nursing team provides expert, round-the-clock medical care tailored to each resident's individual health needs. We maintain rigorous standards through internal auditing and regular inspections.

  • 24/7 qualified nursing staff on-site
  • Individual care plans developed on admission
  • Regular reviews with residents and families
  • Medication management and clinical oversight
  • Specialist care coordination with NHS professionals
  • Fully registered with Care Inspectorate Scotland
02

Activities & Lifestyle

Our dedicated lifestyle leader organises a rich and varied programme of activities designed to stimulate, entertain, and connect our residents with each other and the wider community.

  • Daily social, creative, and therapeutic activities
  • Music, arts, reminiscence sessions
  • Gardening and outdoor pursuits
  • Community outings and local events
  • Intergenerational activities and programmes
  • Tailored individual activity plans
03

Nutritious Dining

Our catering manager and team of dedicated cooks prepare home-cooked, nutritious meals from fresh ingredients every day. Dining at Bo'ness is a pleasure , a social occasion as much as a meal.

  • Fresh home-cooked meals daily
  • Menus tailored to dietary requirements
  • Special diets and preferences accommodated
  • Snacks and refreshments available throughout the day
  • Beautiful, dedicated dining room
  • Nutritional oversight by care team
04

Hair & Wellness

Looking and feeling well is central to a happy life. Our on-site hairdressing salon and visiting wellness professionals help residents maintain their personal style and wellbeing.

  • On-site hair stylist for cuts, sets and styling
  • Qualified chiropodist visits regularly
  • Complementary therapy sessions available
  • Personalised grooming and personal care support
  • Nail care and beauty treatments
  • Wellbeing support as part of care planning
05

Family & Community

We warmly welcome families and friends at any time. We believe the relationships our residents hold most dear are a vital part of their wellbeing, and we do everything we can to nurture those connections.

  • Open visiting , no restrictions on hours
  • Family involvement in care planning and reviews
  • Regular family update meetings
  • Community events and seasonal celebrations
  • Close links with local healthcare and community services
  • Proximity to Bo'ness town centre and transport
