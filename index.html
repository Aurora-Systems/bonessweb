Our Services

Comprehensive Care
for Every Need

From nursing care to wellness activities, we offer a full spectrum of services designed to support health, happiness, and independence.

Nursing Care

Round-the-clock qualified nursing staff delivering personalised medical care, with care plans reviewed and adapted regularly to meet evolving needs.

Activities Programme

Our lifestyle leader organises a rich and varied programme of social, creative, and therapeutic activities to keep residents engaged and fulfilled.

Beautiful Surroundings

Private mature gardens with a traditional bandstand, inviting lounges, and peaceful sitting rooms , creating a true home environment.

Hair & Wellness

On-site hair stylist and qualified chiropodist, alongside complementary therapies, ensuring residents look and feel their very best.

Nutritious Dining

Home-cooked meals prepared fresh daily by our catering team, tailored to dietary needs and presented to delight , dining as a daily pleasure.

Family Partnership

We welcome visitors at any time and actively involve residents and their families in care planning , because great care is a shared endeavour.
